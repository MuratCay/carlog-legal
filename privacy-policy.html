Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This app is a car maintenance log. It is built so that your data stays yours. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app stores, where, and who else sees any of it.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No servers of ours. We do not run a backend and cannot see your data.
  • No ads, no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no tracking.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter: your vehicles, service records, costs, odometer readings, maintenance schedules and reminders, fuel and charging entries, documents and their expiry dates, and any photos you attach (for example receipts). The app also keeps a few settings, such as your units.

Where it is stored

  • On your iPhone. Your log is saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • In your own iCloud, if sync is on. iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Data. When it is on, your log is copied to the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, using Apple's CloudKit. Photos are uploaded as CloudKit assets in the same private database. Only you can read that database; we have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Turning sync off never deletes your data; the copy on your iPhone stays.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. To check whether you have Premium, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt and a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app. It does not receive your name, email, Apple Account, or anything you have entered in the app. Like any network request, its requests carry basic technical details such as the app version and iOS version. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple processes the payment itself; we never see your payment details.

Exports and backups

Your data leaves the app in a file only when you ask for it: a backup file, a CSV export or a PDF service history. You choose where that file goes (for example Files, AirDrop or email). Before some data-changing actions the app saves a safety backup in the app's folder in the Files app on your device.

Notifications

Maintenance and document reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you pick, not your library.

Deleting your data

Delete a vehicle or record in the app, or use Settings → Data → Delete All Data. Deleting the app removes the data on your iPhone. If sync was on, you can also remove the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
